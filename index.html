Strategic Evaluation Matrix

Solar Decisions Should Be Made on Fit, Not Fashion

A direct commercial comparison answering the most frequent boardroom buying dilemmas.

Strategic Dimension
Option A
Option B
Rooftop vs Ground-Mount
Rooftop: Ideal for facilities with unshaded PEB/RCC roofs; zero land cost, direct captive consumption.
Ground-Mount: Ideal when land is available; allows higher multi-MW scale and optimized tilt geometry.
CAPEX vs OPEX / RESCO
CAPEX: You own the asset; 100% of power savings accrue to business; 40% accelerated depreciation tax write-off.
OPEX / PPA: Zero upfront capital investment; pay only for power at a 30-40% discount to grid tariff.
Captive vs Open Access
Captive: Power generated and consumed within premises; zero grid transmission wheeling fees.
Open Access: Off-site plant wheeling power across state DISCOM grids for multi-facility enterprise loads.
On-Site vs Off-Site
On-Site: Rapid commissioning, simple regulatory approvals, maximum energy security on factory premises.
Off-Site: Unlimited scaling potential, unconstrained by factory shed dimensions or warehouse borders.
